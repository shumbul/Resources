Fix theme-toggle tick flash and add reusable practice widgets

Palette icon fix:
- The theme switcher swapped its paint icon to a tick as save feedback, and that
  fired on load while restoring the saved theme, so the tick briefly showed
  alongside/instead of the paint. Removed the tick swap; the paint icon now stays
  constant on load and after selecting a theme (verified across all pages).

New reusable widgets (functions/practice.js, wired into site.js):
- Practice timer: <div class="practice-timer" data-seconds="90"> renders a big
  MM:SS countdown with Start/Pause/Reset, a shrinking bar, a soft WebAudio beep,
  and a flash at zero. Respects reduced-motion.
- Random question picker: <div class="practice-picker" data-source="#list">
  surfaces one random question at a time from a list, shuffled so all appear
  before repeats, with an N/total counter.
- Applied both to the STAR practice section (turning a static wall of 24 into a
  rehearsal tool) and a 90s timer to the Interview Prep Kit behavioral section.
  Also split the vague "goals too high or too low" question into a clean one.

Validated: STAR + Interview Prep WCAG AA 0 violations (desktop + mobile),
0 overflow, picker cycles uniquely, timer counts/resets, HTMLHint 40 files clean.
