Why these exist

A guide is the right shape when you are doing the work. It is the wrong shape when you are trying to remember whether you forgot something, or when you have ten minutes on a bus.

So each map is a checklist you can scan: every branch is a section of the topic, every node is one thing to cover, and the MUST and EDGE chips tell you what is genuinely required versus what makes you stand out. Every branch links back to the guide that explains it properly.

These are also designed to be screenshotted and shared. If one is useful, take it.
