Applying smart (not just often)

  • Apply on the official portal and, in parallel, find a referral on LinkedIn. A referral plus a direct application is the strongest combo.
  • Tailor the top of your resume to each role's keywords. See the Resume guide.
  • Set up alerts on the portals for roles you want, so you apply early.
  • Track your applications in a simple sheet: company, role, date, status, referral.
  • Follow up politely on LinkedIn after applying. See the LinkedIn guide.
