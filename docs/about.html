A little of my journey

I was once the person googling "how do I even start", refreshing job portals, and wondering if I was behind. I wasn't, and neither are you. Consistent, small steps got me from a CS student at NITK to shipping software at Microsoft, and the same approach is what these guides are built around.

If any of this helps you land something or just feel less lost, that's the whole point. That would genuinely make my day.
