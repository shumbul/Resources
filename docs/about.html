👋 About

Free, friendly, no-nonsense tech-career resources. Built by someone who remembers exactly how confusing the start can be.

Hi, I'm Shumbul 👋

I'm a tech enthusiast who loves helping people break into the industry. Somewhere along my own journey I realized how much scattered, gate-kept, or overwhelming advice is out there, and how little of it is actually practical for a beginner. So I started collecting the guides I wish I'd had: clear, honest, and free.

Everything here is written to be read on a coffee break and acted on the same day.

What this site is

A growing library of career and skill guides, roadmaps for learning, LinkedIn and resume help, DSA and system design starters, interview prep, and even a built-in AI helper that reads each page and answers your questions right there.

🆓
Always free

No paywalls, no email gates, no upsells. Share it with anyone.

🌱
Beginner-first

Written for freshers and career-switchers, not for people who already know.

🛠️
Practical

Templates, checklists, and examples you can use today, not just theory.

How to use it

  • Browse the guides in the sidebar and start with whatever feels most urgent for you.
  • New to everything? Begin with the 12-Week Roadmap.
  • Job hunting now? Jump to The AI Era Job Hunt and Resume & Portfolio.
  • Stuck on any page? Tap ✦ Ask AI at the bottom-right, it's free and reads the page for context.
