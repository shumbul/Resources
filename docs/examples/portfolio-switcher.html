Log triage script that the whole team now uses

At work · Built over 3 weekends · Used by 11 people

Every incident started with the same twenty minutes of grepping logs across four servers. I wrote a script that pulls the relevant window, groups errors by signature, and prints the three most likely causes based on our past incidents.

First-response time on P2 incidents dropped from about 25 minutes to under 10. It is now in the team runbook.

About the switch

I did mechanical engineering, joined an IT services company because that was the job available, and ended up in production support. For a long time I read that as a dead end. It is not. Five years of being on the receiving end of other people's bugs taught me what fragile code looks like before it fails, and I have never met a developer who thinks about 3 AM as much as I do.

What I am upfront about: I have not shipped production features in a product team. I am applying for roles where that is understood, and where being genuinely good at debugging is worth something on day one.

Studying at night, mostly backend and systems. Reach me at vikram@example.com.
