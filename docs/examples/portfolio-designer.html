Patient intake redesign

Cutting a 40 minute intake form to 9 minutes

Lead designer · 5 months · Healthcare platform

Nurses were filling the same patient details three times across two systems. I sat in a clinic for four days before drawing anything, then rebuilt the flow around what they actually did rather than what the form assumed.

Intake time down 78%. Nurse-reported errors down by half in the first quarter.

Design system

A design system three teams actually used

Lead designer · 8 months · With two engineers

The previous system failed because it shipped as a Figma file with no code. This time we built components and documentation together, and I ran a weekly clinic where anyone could bring a screen and get help.

Adoption reached 80% of new screens in six months. Design review time halved.

Onboarding

Onboarding for people who do not trust apps

Solo designer · 3 months · Fintech

Our users were first-time investors and dropping off at identity verification. Research showed it was not confusion, it was suspicion. We redesigned around explaining why each document was needed.

Verification completion up from 52% to 79%.
